Data Analyst

We are looking for a Data Analyst with 3+ years of experience to turn data into insights for our business teams.

Responsibilities:
- Analyze large datasets using SQL and Python (pandas) to find trends and opportunities.
- Build dashboards and reports in Power BI or Tableau; track KPIs and present findings to stakeholders.
- Design and maintain ETL processes and data models.
- Run statistical analysis, forecasting and A/B testing.

Requirements:
- Bachelor's degree in Statistics, Computer Science, Engineering or related field.
- Strong Excel, SQL and data visualization skills.
- Excellent communication and presentation skills.
